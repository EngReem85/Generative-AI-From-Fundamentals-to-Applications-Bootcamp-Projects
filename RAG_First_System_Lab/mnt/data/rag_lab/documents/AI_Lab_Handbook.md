# AI Lab Handbook

## AI Lab duration
The standard AI Lab session lasts 120 minutes.

The recommended structure is 20 minutes of explanation, 70 minutes of hands-on implementation, and 30 minutes of testing and reflection.

## Final project
The final project should demonstrate a working AI feature inside a small application. A notebook-only submission is not considered a complete product demonstration.

## Support
Learners can ask technical questions during the weekly support hour. The support hour is held every Wednesday from 6:00 PM to 7:00 PM.
